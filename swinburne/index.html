About

A coordinator who turns research and relationships into delivered campaigns.

I like the part of this work where a research finding becomes a clear point of view, that point of view becomes an integrated campaign, and the campaign lands on brief, on brand and on deadline. Coordinating the people and moving parts to get there is the job I’m best at.

I’ve done it across very different audiences and stakeholders — members, media, commercial partners and fans at Melbourne Storm; founders, agencies and vendors at Chat Response; product, engineering and leadership at GolfTrak. I focus on what each stakeholder actually needs, then find the version of a plan that gets everyone to a shared outcome.
