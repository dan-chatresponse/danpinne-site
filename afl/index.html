About

My background sits between production, product and audience.

I enjoy taking a messy queue of requests and turning it into shipped work: scoped, prioritised, QA’d and reported on. The best digital production is invisible — fans just see content that arrives on time, apps that work on match day, and campaigns that land.

I move comfortably between roadmap and reality: writing user stories and shipping sprints one week, analysing support conversations, reviews and behavioural trends the next. I’ve worked in lean startup environments where speed and iteration mattered more than process, and in established sporting organisations where audience scale — and match-day pressure — was the game.

Lately that’s meant taking GolfTrak from a six-year build to public launch, running delivery across app, web, portal, email and support channels, and using AI to speed up research, documentation, testing and delivery planning.
