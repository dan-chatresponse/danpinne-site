About

My background sits between digital, commerce and purpose.

I like the part of this work where strategy becomes a journey, a journey becomes a well-timed message, and that message becomes an action a person actually takes — a subscription, a purchase, or a donation. The mechanics are the same whether the outcome is revenue or a cure; the difference is what the result pays for.

I’m equally comfortable setting digital strategy with leadership and getting into the detail of a supporter journey or a conversion report. At GolfTrak that means owning growth across product, CRM and lifecycle; at 24/7 Golf it meant rebuilding an entire funnel; at Melbourne Storm it meant building a digital team and a revenue line from nothing.

FightMND is where that mix has the most meaning. Every improvement to the supporter experience helps fund research, support families and bring the fight against MND one step closer to a win.
