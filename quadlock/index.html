About

Strategy and data on one side, hands in Klaviyo on the other.

I like the part of retention where a cohort chart points at exactly where customers lapse, and a flow fixes it — then the next one. I back myself to form recommendations from the numbers rather than waiting to be told, and I keep the database healthy so those numbers stay trustworthy.

SMS, used well, is one of the most powerful retention channels there is — high intent, low frequency, genuinely personal. I’ve used automated SMS and direct messaging to open conversations and drive real lifecycle actions, not just blast promos.

I’m an active, outdoors dad — golf, the ocean, wild places with my kids. Building retention for products people are passionate about using is exactly where I want to be.
