The four things this role runs on: managing the people who commission and create the work, and publishing it on time, on brand and on the platforms fans actually live on.

01

Client, sponsor & athlete management

I act as the link between clients, sponsors, athletes and fans — translating briefs into creative concepts, making sure sponsor deliverables land accurately and on time, and supporting athletes as they build their own profiles.

02

Content commissioning & production

I build content strategies, editorial calendars, briefs, scripts and shot lists — then oversee creative from concept through to delivery. I’m hands-on with short-form video, copy and photography when it’s faster to shoot it than brief it.

03

People & freelancer management

I brief, direct and mentor freelance creators, review their work against the brief, and keep production inside budget — built from a decade of running a contractor network and growing an in-house club team.

04

Publishing, paid social & reporting

I plan, schedule and publish across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn, run paid social on Meta, Google and TikTok, and report on what actually moved audience and commercial outcomes.

About

My background sits between content, sport and audience.

I like the part of this work where a brief becomes a concept, a concept becomes a shoot, and the shoot becomes something a fan actually stops scrolling for. Sport is the best version of that job — the audience is already emotionally invested, so the only real question is whether the content is good enough and fast enough.

I’m equally comfortable briefing a freelancer and picking up the camera myself. At Storm that meant training sessions, player content, match day and sponsor deliverables in the same week; at GolfTrak it means demos, tutorials and paid social that has to earn its budget back.

Women’s sport is at the point where audience growth is limited more by content capacity than by interest — which is exactly the gap this role exists to close. Three clubs, three fan bases, one content engine.
