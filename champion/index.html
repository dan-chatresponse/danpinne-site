About

Technical enough for the detail, commercial enough for the room.

I like the part of product where an ambiguous problem becomes a spec a developer can build from, and that build becomes something customers and partners actually rely on. I’ve done that across apps, web platforms and data products — and signed off the releases at the end of it.

My background is genuinely sports-tech and broadcast media: GolfTrak’s computer-vision performance data, and Melbourne Storm’s digital, video and match-day platforms. I know how clubs, media partners and fans consume sports data, which is exactly the world Champion Data operates in.

I’m as comfortable running a demo for a customer as I am maintaining a product register and deciding what to retire — the whole lifecycle, not just the build.
