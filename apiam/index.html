About

My work sits between data, journeys and genuine care.

I like the part of this work where a lifecycle stage becomes a well-timed message, and that message becomes a customer who stays. The mechanics are the same across industries; what changes is what the relationship is worth — and for Apiam it’s the care of animals and the communities around them.

I’m equally comfortable mapping a journey, building the Klaviyo flow and cleaning the data underneath it. Automated SMS and direct messaging are a core part of how I lift response — used well, as a high-value channel rather than noise.

Purpose-led organisations are where I do my best work. Apiam’s national network is exactly the kind of place I want to put a decade of lifecycle experience behind.
